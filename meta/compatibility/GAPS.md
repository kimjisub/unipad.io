Written 2026-09-30 from `data/support_matrix.csv` and `output/COVERAGE_SUMMARY.md`. Nothing here is a decision to add a screen, raise or
lower a supported version, or support a device, and nothing here is a promise to users; each line is only the next smallest check that would
turn a `TODO:` row into evidence.

Order: (1) rows where what the project says and what the platform offers disagree, (2) breadth of the affected share in the named proxy
series, (3) how little the check needs. Every figure is a percentage of that one series' own table for the stated period (StatCounter: page
views, August 2026; Apple: devices that used the App Store on 2026-06-07). The series must not be added together, and none of them counts
people or installed devices. No number of affected users is estimated anywhere in this list.
