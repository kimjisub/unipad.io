## In short

- UniPad has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we can't tell who you are from what the app sends.
- The app sends five kinds of data to Google Firebase, which runs these services for us: rough location (country or city, worked out from your IP address), how you use the app, crash reports, diagnostics, and random IDs for your install.
- We use this to find and fix crashes and to see which features work. One ID also lets us send notifications.
- We don't sell or share this data, and current versions of the app show no ads.
- Everything is sent encrypted.

## What the apps collect

| Data | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rough location | Country, region or city, worked out by Google from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address itself. The apps never use GPS or ask for location permission. | Analytics: which countries and languages to support |
| App interactions | App opens, screens you visit, and UniPack events: importing or loading a pack, starting and ending play, the first input (pad or auto-play). With them we record the result (success, failure, cancelled), where a pack came from (file or store), the error type, a range for how long you played, screen orientation, a screen size range and window mode. Never pack titles, file names or anything you type. | Analytics: to see what works and what fails |
| Crash logs | When the app crashes: where in the code it stopped, the app version, device model, OS version, free memory and storage, the screen or play state at the time, how the app was installed, and the reason the system gave when it closed the app. | To find and fix crashes |
| Diagnostics | Reports of errors that didn't crash the app (for example a failed save), with the same technical details as crash logs. | To find and fix problems |
| Device or other IDs | A random ID created for each install (Firebase installation ID), and a notification token. See "Advertising ID" below. | Analytics; the notification token is for sending notifications (app functionality) |

Android and iOS collect the same kinds of data.

### Advertising ID

- **Android 4.2.1 and earlier:** Google Analytics in these versions reads the Android advertising ID and sends it with the analytics data. We don't use it for ads.
- **Android, from the update after 4.2.1:** the app no longer reads or sends the advertising ID.
- **iOS:** the app never reads the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendors for analytics, and it doesn't ask to track you.

You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

### Old versions with ads (3.x)

UniPad versions before 4.0 showed ads from Google AdMob. If you still use one of those versions, Google collects data such as the advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads, under Google's policy: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Updating to the current version removes the ads.

### What we don't collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, payment data or account. The UniPacks you import and play stay on your device; we don't upload them. There are no purchases in the app.

## Notifications

To send notifications (such as news about UniPad), the app gets a notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging. On iOS and on Android 13 and later, notifications appear only if you allow them. You can turn them off in your device's notification settings at any time.

## Downloads and updates

- When you download a UniPack from the store in the app, your device connects to our pack file server (unipack.unipad.io), which we run with a hosting provider in South Korea. On the website, the download goes through Vercel to the same server. Like any web server, it records each request, including the IP address that made it, to keep the server running; we keep these records for no more than 30 days.
- On Android, the app checks Google Play for app updates. Google Play handles that check under Google's own policy; we don't receive that data.

## The website (unipad.io)

- unipad.io uses Google Analytics (pages visited, rough location, browser and device type, and errors on the page). Google Analytics stores cookies in your browser for this. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site still works.
- It also uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies.
- Packs you open in the web player and the store list cache are saved only in your browser's storage. You can clear them in your browser settings.

## Who handles the data

We don't sell or share your data. Google runs the services that receive it on our behalf (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging and Google Cloud) and may process it on servers in the United States and other countries. Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Vercel hosts the website: [https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). UniPack downloads are handled by our own pack file server in South Korea (see "Downloads and updates").

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Analytics events linked to an install ID: no more than 14 months. Reports made only of totals (for example "users per country") may be kept longer.
- Crash reports: 90 days.
- Download server logs: no more than 30 days.
- Install IDs and notification tokens: until the app is removed or they stop being used.

## Your choices

- The app has no switch to turn off analytics or crash reports. You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app.
- Clearing the app's data or reinstalling it creates a new random install ID, so new data can't be linked to the old data.
- Turn off notifications in your device settings.
- On Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Deleting your data and your rights

Because there are no accounts and we don't collect names or contact details, we have no way to find which data belongs to you, so we can't delete it on request. The data deletes itself after the periods above, and reinstalling the app starts a new ID.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom, South Korea or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data. Our legal basis for analytics and crash reports is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it; for notifications it is your permission. For any question or request, email [0226unipad@gmail.com](mailto:0226unipad@gmail.com). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

UniPad isn't directed at children under 13. The apps collect the same limited, nameless data from everyone and nothing that identifies a person. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.
