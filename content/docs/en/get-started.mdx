## Get UniPad

- **Web**: [Play in your browser](/play) with nothing to install.
- **Android**: [Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kimjisub.launchpad)
- **iOS**: [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760479102)

## Check your version and update

- The app shows its version near the logo on the main screen and in **Settings › Information**.
- Google Play rolls out each new version to a portion of users first and then widens it, so the version you see may differ from another device for a while. New versions are not offered on devices below Android 7.0.
- On the App Store you can get the latest version at any time.

## Play your first pack

1. **Get a pack.**
   - In the app, tap **Download new packs** to use the Store, or **Import downloaded packs** for a .zip you already have.
   - On the web, use **Browse Store** or **Import UniPack**, or drag a .zip file onto the player.
2. Tap the pack in the list, then tap **Play**.
3. **No Launchpad?** Tap the pads on screen. On the web you can also use your keyboard:

| Keys | Pad row |
|------|---------|
| `1` – `8` | Row 1 |
| `Q` – `I` | Row 2 |
| `A` – `K` | Row 3 |
| `Z` – `,` | Row 4 |
| `Shift` + the keys above | Rows 5 – 8 |

## Want to make your own pack?

UniPad has no pack editor. A pack is a .zip file that bundles sound files with a few text files describing which sound and light each pad plays. See the [UniPack documentation](/docs/unipack) for the format, and share your work or ask questions on [Discord](https://discord.gg/ESDgyNs).
